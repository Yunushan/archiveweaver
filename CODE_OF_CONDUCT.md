# Code of conduct

ArchiveWeaver contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces safe,
professional, and focused on reliable operations. Treat people with respect,
assume good faith while examining technical claims critically, and welcome
different levels of experience and perspective.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private
information, or pressuring anyone to disclose credentials, customer data, or
security-sensitive operational details.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers,
project events, and private communication conducted on behalf of the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject changes, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community or its users. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private
contact method on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue. Report
software vulnerabilities through the separate private process in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and
retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.
